⚠️ Priority booking actions

Confirm the core bookings and trip arrangements

  • Confirm any hire-car pickup and return arrangements
    Action required
    If a vehicle is required, confirm the supplier, pickup and return locations, times, driver requirements, payment and insurance before departure.
  • Confirm the outbound and return flights for every traveller
    Flights
    Recheck passenger names, travel dates, airports, flight times, seating, meals and the booked baggage allowance.
  • Confirm all accommodation bookings for the trip
    Accommodation
    Confirm each property, stay date, room, guest name, check-in time, payment status and reservation reference for 30 Aug to 4 Sep.
  • Confirm every traveller has suitable active insurance
    Insurance
    Confirm the policy covers Fiji for 30 Aug to 4 Sep 2026. Save each policy, PDS, claims details and emergency number offline.

🛂 Entry, immigration and customs

Check the requirements that apply to every traveller

  • Check every passport and carry it in hand luggage
    Passport
    Confirm each passport is valid well beyond 4 Sep 2026 and meets the entry and transit requirements that apply to the traveller.
  • Reconfirm visa-free visitor entry one week before departure
    Entry
    Recheck Fiji entry and transit requirements for each traveller’s passport. Keep the return flight, confirmed accommodation address and access to sufficient funds ready for Immigration.
  • Complete a Fiji Passenger Arrival Card for each traveller
    On arrival
    Complete both sides before landing and use the address of the first confirmed accommodation.
  • Review customs and biosecurity declarations before packing
    Declare when unsure
    Declare food of any kind, plant or animal products, seeds, honey, used outdoor equipment and other biosecurity-risk goods. Declare currency of FJ$10,000 or more, or the foreign-currency equivalent.
  • Prepare documents for prescription or controlled medicines
    Medicines
    Carry medicines in original packaging with the prescription or a doctor’s letter confirming they are medically necessary and for personal use.

🚗 Hire car and road travel

Documents, payment and offline navigation

  • If hiring a car, pack the required driver licence and IDP
    Driver ID
    Confirm the supplier’s licence requirements and keep the physical licence and any required International Driving Permit in carry-on luggage.
  • Save the hire-car booking and protection documents offline
    Rental booking
    Include the booking confirmation, protection or insurance documents, collection map and supplier contact details.
  • Prepare the payment card and any remaining rental balance
    Payment
    Confirm the outstanding balance, accepted card, security-deposit requirement, fuel policy and whether the named driver must present the card.
  • Download offline maps and save all confirmed trip locations
    Navigation
    Save the airports, accommodation, transport collection points and planned activity locations before leaving Australia.
  • Photograph the vehicle at pickup and confirm return conditions
    At pickup
    Record all panels, wheels, windscreen, fuel level and odometer before driving away. Confirm the drop-off location, after-hours process and emergency contact.

📱 Money, phone and offline access

Payment resilience and access without mobile data

  • Check bank cards, limits and PINs
    Money
    Ensure the main and backup cards are active for Fiji, have sufficient limits for hotels and car hire, and are stored separately.
  • Arrange a modest amount of Fiji dollars for arrival
    FJD cash
    Carry enough for small purchases and unexpected card issues; use cards for larger transactions where practical.
  • Install and test the Vodafone Fiji eSIM before departure
    Connectivity
    Save the Account Login, User Manual and Support details offline. Keep the Australian SIM active for messages but disable its mobile-data roaming.
  • Download a complete offline travel-document set
    Offline
    Save flight, accommodation, hire-car and insurance documents, passport copies, emergency contacts and key screenshots to both phones.
  • Pack chargers, cables and a charged power bank
    Power
    Fiji uses the same Type I plug as Australia, so an adaptor is normally unnecessary for Australian devices.

🧳 Packing and health

Only the essential Fiji-specific checks

  • Weigh every checked bag and carry-on
    Booked limits
    Check each traveller’s booked carry-on and checked-baggage allowance and leave room for purchases on the return flight.
  • Prepare one complete carry-on essentials set
    Carry-on
    Passports, licences and IDPs, medicines, chargers, insurance details, valuables, arrival-card pens and one change of clothes.
  • Pack tropical-weather and mosquito protection
    Fiji
    Include sunscreen, hats, insect repellent, light rain protection, swimwear, suitable footwear and a light layer for air-conditioned spaces.

✈️ Final 48 hours and travel day

Check-in, airport timing and the last departure sweep

  • Check flight status and complete online check-in
    48 hours
    Save boarding passes to each phone and take screenshots. Reconfirm terminals, baggage rules and any schedule changes for both flights.
  • Confirm the departure-airport plan
    30 Aug
    Confirm the departure terminal, transport to the airport and the airline’s recommended international check-in time.
  • Complete the final home and luggage sweep
    Before leaving
    Check doors, windows, appliances, bins, mail and keys; then confirm passports, phones, wallets and medicines before departure.
  • Lock in the return-day sequence
    4 Sep
    Confirm accommodation checkout, airport transport, any vehicle return, the departure terminal and the required international check-in time.
